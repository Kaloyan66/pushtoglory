      Most outreach gets opened and then ignored. Not because the offer is weak. Because the email is too long, full of industry words, and all about the sender.

      This workflow fixes all three. It turns whatever you sell into short, human emails built on a structure that was tested, broken, rebuilt from real recipient feedback and proven to earn replies. Each one reads in five seconds on a phone.

      What it doesWhat you get

      • ✓Finished emails, one per prospect, each written around the business you are contacting
      • ✓A structure that leads with them, never with you
      • ✓Plain words only. No jargon, no acronyms, nothing that sounds like a brochure
      • ✓A free offer in every email, the smallest slice of your work, so the reply costs them nothing
      • ✓Built in quality rules that check every email before you see it
      • ✓Variety across a batch, so fifty emails to the same industry never look cloned

      Who it is forAny business that sells to other people

      Agencies, freelancers, consultants, recruiters, designers, trades, local services, software, coaching. If you sell something to someone, it works. Nothing in it is tied to one market.

      No writing experience needed. No marketing background needed. If you have a sample of your work, the emails include it. If you do not yet, it switches to a shorter version built for people just starting out.
