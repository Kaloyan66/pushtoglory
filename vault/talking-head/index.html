      One creator, talking straight to camera about your product, in a way that feels like a real person filmed it on their phone. Two clips of the same person from two angles, stitched together, with a clean product B-roll cut in and subtitles on top.

      The hard part of AI talking heads was never the first clip. It is the second one. A 15 second limit per generation means every real ad is at least two clips, and the moment you render the second one the face shifts, the outfit changes and the room rearranges itself. This template exists to stop that.

      What it doesWhat you get

      • ✓Two stitchable talking head prompts, Video 1 and Video 2, with every locked block identical
      • ✓A product B-roll prompt that hides the join and carries the clean product shot
      • ✓The attachments list: which reference image and which voice goes on which clip
      • ✓The voice lock, so your creator sounds like one person across both clips
      • ✓The edit order and caption rules that make two generations feel like one video
      • ✓The fixes for the four things that go wrong, as copy and paste instructions

      How it worksYou describe it, Claude fills the template

      Everything runs on one locked prompt template that installs into Claude. You describe the product, the creator, the setting and the vibe in plain words. Claude fills every placeholder and returns prompts you paste straight into the generator. No prompt writing, no guessing at structure.

      It is universal. There is no house look built in: any product, any setting, any style, from a selfie in a car to a locked off shot at a desk. One purchase covers every campaign you run from here.
