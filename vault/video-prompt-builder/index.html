      Most video prompts are a paragraph of adjectives. The model fills every gap with a guess, and you pay for the guesses in re-rolls. This skill writes the prompt the way a director writes a shot list: every shot timed, every camera move named, every transition decided.

      You describe the video in one or two lines. "A guy leaves a tumbler in a hot car and it is still cold" is enough. Claude reads the brief, finds the visual device that proves the claim, and hands back a prompt you paste straight into Seedance.

      What it doesWhat you get back, every time

      • ✓A premise note that names the one creative decision the whole video hangs on, so you can push back before anything renders
      • ✓A shot by shot timeline with timecodes, effects, camera move, speed and an exit for every shot
      • ✓The signature shot, marked: the one shot that is the entire ad in three seconds
      • ✓A master effects inventory with counts and the reason each effect is there
      • ✓An effects density map, so the video has rhythm instead of a flat wall of effects
      • ✓An energy arc broken into acts, so the video lands instead of stopping
      • ✓Generator settings and production notes, including what to do when the key shot fails
      • ✓Identity and product locks in every prompt, the main reason AI ads fall apart

      Where it fitsThe last mile of every video workflow

      The Seedance 2.0 Film System gives you the producer layer for free: story, continuity, the scene map and one brief per generation. This is the prompt layer it hands off to. The free skill literally stops at the prompts and asks for it.

      It also works on its own. Product spots, brand films, ad concepts, anything you want to see move. Give it a claim instead of a scene ("our shoe is light", not "show a shoe on a table") and it builds the whole video around proving it.

      What is insideInside Premium

      • The full guide: how to install it, how to brief it, how to read what it returns, how reference tags work, how to choose length and model, and how to rescue a signature shot that will not render
      • The quick start as a Word document you can keep next to you
      • Every update, whenever Seedance changes and the skill changes with it

      It is for anyone who already makes AI video and is tired of re-rolling, and for anyone starting out who wants their first prompts to look like they came from someone who has done this a hundred times.
