The Social Post Writer

Turns a finished asset or a rough idea into publish ready posts, shaped correctly for every platform you name. Any niche, any voice.

      You made something worth posting. Now it needs to become a LinkedIn post, an X post, an Instagram caption and a Facebook version, each shaped for how that room actually reads. This skill does that in one pass, from a photo, a result, a link or one rough sentence.

      It is built on one rule that decides quality: a post that tells the audience something they already know is a failed post, no matter how well written it is. So before it writes a word, it hunts for the angle a typical reader in your niche would not already have.

      What it doesWhat you get

      • ✓One post per platform you name, rebuilt for that platform rather than cut down from another one
      • ✓Ten platforms covered: LinkedIn, X, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Threads, Bluesky, YouTube, Pinterest and Reddit
      • ✓The right shape before the words: seven post types, from announcements to stories to offers
      • ✓Your voice, matched from a sample or a past post when you give one
      • ✓No invented numbers. It never makes up results or metrics, and tells you exactly where to drop your real ones
      • ✓Copy that pastes cleanly, with paragraph spacing that survives the trip into each app
      • ✓Your house rules remembered: emoji or none, hashtags or none, no em dashes, words that are off limits

      Who it is forAny niche

      Local businesses, SaaS, coaches, ecommerce, restaurants, fitness, real estate, nonprofits, creators, agencies and personal brands. The skill supplies the craft and the platform rules. You supply the subject and the voice.

      Paste a competitor's post as a style reference and it keeps only the skeleton: the hook type, the order, where the ask sits. Their claims and their subject are thrown away.

      • Example briefs you can copy for your first posts
