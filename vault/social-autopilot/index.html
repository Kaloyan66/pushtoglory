The Social Media Autopilot

Post and schedule videos, pictures and text on Facebook, Instagram, X and LinkedIn from one folder on your computer. No API keys, no developer accounts, no monthly fees, and nothing half made ever goes out.

      Posting one thing to four platforms means four composers, four upload bars, four caption rewrites and four scheduling calendars that all work differently. Every single week. This turns it into one folder and one double click.

      Each post is a folder. Inside it, a caption file with an optional shorter version per platform, and one picture or video. Add a date and the post is scheduled inside Facebook, Instagram, X and LinkedIn themselves. It sits in each platform's own scheduled list, and your computer can be switched off afterwards.

      What it doesWhat it handles for you

      • ✓Four platforms from one folder: Facebook, Instagram, X and LinkedIn
      • ✓Native scheduling. Posts wait in each platform's own scheduled list, so your computer does not have to be on when the time comes
      • ✓One caption, or one per platform. A shorter X version, a longer LinkedIn version, everything else shared
      • ✓The made with AI label, switched on for you on Facebook, Instagram and X, for every post or only the ones you mark
      • ✓Nothing half made goes out. Before the final button it confirms the caption is really in the box, the upload finished, the AI label reads on and the schedule time reads back correctly. Any check fails, it stops and saves a screenshot
      • ✓Never posts twice. Everything sent is recorded, so running it again skips it
      • ✓A rehearsal mode that fills in every composer and photographs it without publishing anything
      • ✓No API keys, no developer apps, no subscription. It uses your own accounts in your own browser

      How it worksYour own browser, signed in as you

      You sign in to each platform once, by hand, in a normal Chrome window. The system never types or stores your password. After that it reuses the saved session in its own private browser profile and does what you would do: opens the composer, attaches the file, types the caption line by line so the paragraph breaks survive, switches on the right settings and presses the button.

      Because it works through the platforms' own composers, a post it schedules is exactly the same as one you scheduled by hand. You can see it, edit it or delete it inside the platform.

      Where it fitsWrite once, publish everywhere

      The Social Post Writer writes one version of a post per platform. This publishes them. Its output drops straight into the platform sections of the caption file, so an idea becomes four scheduled posts without opening a single composer.

      Before you buyHonest limits

      • Windows 10 or 11 only. It runs from double click buttons built for Windows.
      • Two free installs. Google Chrome and Python. The guide walks you through both, and setup takes about fifteen minutes, once.
      • Your accounts must be set to English. It finds the buttons by their English names.
      • Facebook schedules for Pages only, not personal profiles. Instagram schedules from Professional accounts, which are free to switch to, and always needs a picture or video. LinkedIn has no made with AI setting, so the label cannot be applied there.
      • One picture or one video per post. No carousels.
      • The platforms' caption limits apply: X 280, Instagram 2,200, LinkedIn 3,000, Facebook 5,000. Over a limit, that platform is skipped with a message. Nothing is ever cut mid sentence.
      • Logins expire every few weeks. Reconnecting is one menu option.
      • Your computer is on while it posts. Once a post is scheduled on the platform, it can be off.
      • It automates your own normal use of these websites. When a platform redesigns a page, it stops safely with a screenshot rather than posting something wrong. Stay within each platform's rules and post at a human pace.
